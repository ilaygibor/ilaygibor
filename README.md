# Ilay Gibor

Co-founder of **Kinetic**, a calisthenics app for skill progression and training. Harvard undergraduate studying mathematics and computer science.

I work across product development, engineering, and user acquisition. My earlier projects include a gym accountability app, liver image-analysis tools, FTC robots, and a community heritage map.

## Selected work

- **[GymPal](https://github.com/ilaygibor/ilaygibor.github.io/tree/main/projects/gympal)**: an iPhone gym-accountability app I co-build with Harel. Mascot widgets, workout commitments, and friend consistency tracking.
- **[Liver image analysis](https://github.com/ilaygibor/ilaygibor.github.io/tree/main/projects/liver-research)**: Python image processing and ANSYS mesh mapping from my research at Long Island University.
- **[FTC robotics](https://github.com/ilaygibor/ilaygibor.github.io/tree/main/projects/robotics)**: engineering portfolios from RoboSapiens and SAR2-D2, where I served as a captain.
- **[Jewish heritage map](https://github.com/ilaygibor/ilaygibor.github.io/tree/main/projects/jewish-heritage)**: a collaborative Google Maps prototype, second place at KoHack 2025.
- **[Battle Cats Discord bot](https://github.com/ilaygibor/ilaygibor.github.io/tree/main/projects/battle-cats-bot)**: an archived statistics/reference bot for our YouTube community.
